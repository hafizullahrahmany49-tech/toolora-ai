Registered Agent Services: What LLC Owners Should Know

Understand the role of a registered agent, the questions to ask before hiring a provider, and how to compare recurring costs without being misled by headline prices.

Important: This guide is for general education. State rules differ, and this page is not a recommendation of any specific provider.

What is a registered agent?

A registered agent is a person or service designated to receive certain official notices and legal documents for a business. States commonly require an LLC or corporation to maintain an agent with a physical street address in the state of registration. Check the specific state's rules for eligibility and responsibilities.

Why might a business hire a service?

  • To have a reliable address for official service of process and state notices.
  • To help keep personal residential details off some public-facing filings where state rules allow.
  • To receive notices during business hours and forward them to the owner.
  • To maintain an in-state agent when the owner lives elsewhere, if the service and state rules permit it.

A registered-agent service does not replace a lawyer, accountant, business address, mailroom, or compliance program unless the contract explicitly includes additional services.

How to compare providers

  • Total price: compare first-year price with renewal price, not just the introductory offer.
  • State coverage: confirm service in the state where your company is registered.
  • Included features: check mail forwarding, document scanning, alerts, and response times.
  • Privacy: understand what information becomes public and what the provider can actually protect.
  • Cancellation: check renewal terms, refunds, and how to change agents.
  • Support: review availability and how urgent legal notices are handled.
  • Extra charges: check annual report, compliance, address, or document fees.

Can a non-US resident use a registered-agent service?

Some providers serve owners who live outside the United States, but availability, identity checks, payment methods, and bundled services vary. Confirm the provider's current eligibility rules directly. Hiring a registered agent does not guarantee LLC approval, an EIN, a US bank account, payment processing, a visa, or a particular tax outcome.

Questions to ask before paying

  • What is the full price for the first year and each renewal?
  • Is the state filing fee included or charged separately?
  • Does the provider accept customers who reside in my country?
  • How are official notices delivered, and how quickly?
  • Can I cancel or switch providers, and what are the terms?
  • Does the package include services I actually need, or only registered-agent service?

Should you choose the cheapest option?

Not automatically. A very low introductory price may renew at a higher rate or exclude useful services. Compare the contract and total costs, and verify the provider's reputation.
